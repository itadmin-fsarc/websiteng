Welcome to FSARC

Our Mission

The mission of the First State Amateur Radio Club (FSARC) is to promote and strengthen the tradition, knowledge, fellowship, and public service of Amateur Radio within New Castle County and throughout the Delaware community. Founded in February/March 1953, FSARC has proudly served Amateur Radio enthusiasts for more than seven decades.

Education

Educational programs and skill-building opportunities for amateur radio operators at all experience levels — from licensing classes to advanced techniques.

Community

Communication support for races, runs, and large-scale public events across Delaware. We serve our community through reliable amateur radio services.

Fellowship

Annual ARRL Field Day, Winter Field Day, fox hunts, and lasting friendships. Join a vibrant community of radio enthusiasts who share your passion.
