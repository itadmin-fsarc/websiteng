About the Event

What is the Delaware QSO Party?

The Delaware QSO Party is an exciting annual contest that invites amateur radio operators from across the country — and beyond — to make contacts with stations in Delaware. Held over a weekend, this event provides a unique opportunity for hams to work all three counties in the First State while testing their operating skills and enjoying the camaraderie of fellow radio enthusiasts.

Whether you're a seasoned contester or a casual operator, the Delaware QSO Party offers a fun and rewarding experience with a variety of operating categories and certificates available for participants. This is also a great event to combine with a park activation (POTA) while you're out in the field!

Stations operating from Delaware play a key role in providing multipliers for participants aiming for top scores. Delaware is often a sought-after state in radio contests, making your participation especially valuable to operators across North America.

All 3 Counties

Work New Castle, Kent, and Sussex County stations for full Delaware coverage.

Certificates Awarded

Multiple operating categories with certificates available for top finishers.

POTA Friendly

Great opportunity to combine your QSO Party operating with a Parks on the Air activation.
